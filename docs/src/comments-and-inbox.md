# Comments and Inbox

This page shows you how to discuss a task with comments, replies and mentions, and how the Inbox tells you when something needs you.

## Comments and replies

Write in the box under a task's **Activity** and press Enter to send. Shift+Enter
starts a new line. A comment can be up to 2,000 characters.

Every project member can comment, reply and mention people, even without the
Board permission. Admins can join in on any project.

- **Reply** sits under each comment. Replies form one thread under the first
  comment; replying to a reply keeps it in that thread and shows who you answered.
- Use a comment's **⋯** menu to **Edit** or **Delete** your own comments.
  Admins can edit or delete any comment. Edited comments are marked "edited".
- A deleted comment shows **Comment removed**, and its replies stay.

## Mentions

1. Type `@` in a comment, a reply or a block reason.
2. Pick a person from the list, or pick **@everyone**.
3. Send as usual.

Only mentions you pick from the list notify anyone. Typing or pasting a name
without picking it is plain text, and so is anything inside `code` or a
quoted line.

**@everyone** notifies every active member of the project except you. Admins
who aren't members aren't included. Before you send, the box shows how many
people will be notified. You can mention everyone once a minute per project.

When you edit a comment, only people you newly mention are notified. Nobody gets
the same @everyone notification twice.

## The Inbox

The Inbox icon sits beside the oneloop logo, and a dot means something is
unread. The Inbox covers all your projects and is private: nobody else, admins
included, can see it.

| What happens | Who is notified |
| --- | --- |
| A mention in a comment or block reason | The person picked |
| @everyone in a comment or block reason | Every active project member |
| A reply | The author of the comment being answered |
| An assignment | Each newly assigned person |
| A task is unblocked | The task's assignees |

Nobody is notified about their own actions. Status changes, edits and new
attachments don't notify anyone.

Each item shows who did what, the task and a short excerpt. Click it to open
the task at the right comment; this also marks it read.

- **Mark read** or **Mark unread**, and **Archive** or **Restore**, work on one item.
- **Mark all as read** and **Archive all** act on everything in the current
  view. With a filter on, they say "filtered" and act only on matches. There is
  no undo, and archived items are restored one at a time.
- Switch between **Inbox** and **Archived**, filter by project, or show only
  **Unread**.

Unread and unarchived items stay until you act on them. Archived items are
removed after 90 days. If you lose access to a project, or a task is deleted,
its items show "This item is no longer available".
