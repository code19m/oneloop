# Your account

This page shows you how to change your profile and password, and how to see and end the places where you're signed in.

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Profile**. The same
menu has **Sign out** and switches this browser between **Light** and **Dark**.

## Profile

- **Avatar:** a PNG, JPEG or WebP image up to 5 MB.
- **Full name:** saved when you leave the field.
- **Username:** can't be changed. Mentions show it, as in `@alex`.

## Password

1. Under **Password**, enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter the **New password** twice. It needs at least 5 characters.
3. Choose **Change password**.

This browser stays signed in. Your other sessions and connected apps are
signed out.

If you forget your password, ask an admin to reset it. You'll get a temporary
password, and oneloop asks you to set a new one when you sign in. The first
sign-in on a new account works the same way.

## Sessions

**Sessions** lists every browser where you're signed in, with its device,
browser, IP address and last activity. **Current** marks this one.

- **Revoke** signs out one session.
- **Sign out other sessions** signs out all but this one. Connected apps keep
  their access.

A session ends after 7 days without use, and after 30 days in any case. You
can have up to 10 at once; at the limit, oneloop asks you to choose one to
sign out before continuing.

## Connected apps

AI assistants you connect over MCP appear under **Connected apps**, with their
projects and permissions. **Revoke** removes an app's access until you connect
it again. See [AI assistants (MCP)](mcp.md).
